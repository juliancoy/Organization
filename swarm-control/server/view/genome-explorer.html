05   Under the hood: sources, methods & limitations

Real counts. Carefully bounded claims.

Julian’s dataset uses prepared GRCh38.p13 calls and an audit of existing VEP annotations. GIAB HG001, HG002 and HG005 are high-confidence autosomal benchmark variants. Watson and Venter are older GRCh37 archives with unknown genotypes and depth. Counts across these sources are not a ranking of health or genetic diversity.

Satellite overlap uses each variant’s reference span against merged GRCh38 RepeatMasker Satellite intervals. It does not estimate satellite abundance, repeat expansions, or centromere completeness. GRCh37 datasets are excluded from this overlap analysis.

Consequence categories overlap: a variant can affect several transcripts. VEP and bcftools csq use different models and must not be compared as equivalent annotations. No clinical interpretations are provided.

This page contains aggregate summaries only. It does not serve reads, genotypes at individual sites, or downloadable genomic files.
